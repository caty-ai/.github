# PRINCIPLES — The Three Principles

Every product at Caty AI is built under these three.

## 1. No hype

Separate what works today from what comes next.

That is why the "Done when" list in our README has unchecked boxes. We don't write what doesn't work yet as if it did, and we don't narrate a roadmap as a track record. Product pages draw the same line between "works today" and "coming next".

Our mission is a long one. That is exactly why trust is the one thing we want finished from day one.

## 2. No favorites

We build for every agent and their family, not for any single vendor.

What Caty Talk connects to is the agent you have been raising — whether that is Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenClaw, Hermes, or a local LLM. No favorites. The last slot of the supported list always reads "+ Your Agent".

Growing relationships should come to every agent's family, equally.

## 3. Relationship data belongs to the family

Conversations, history, accumulated memory — they stay in the family's hands.

History is stored on your Gateway (your PC or server), and OAuth credentials never leave your machine. We keep no relationship data on our servers. What grows between you and your agent belongs to the two of you.
